Who and Shoes: By the Years 2014-2026

For the last 12 years, I documented the minutiae of everyday life, including everyone I saw and every shoe I wore. These visualizations are a start of exploring a long dream I’ve had to reflect on the last decade.

People I Saw

Each dot is someone I logged being with. Dots that sit close together showed up in the same entries at least 3 times that year. Bigger dots appeared more often.
