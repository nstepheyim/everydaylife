Who and Shoes: By the Years 2014-2026

For the last 12 years, I documented the minutiae of everyday life, including everyone I saw and every shoe I wore. These visualizations are a start of exploring a long dream I’ve had to make sense and reflect on the past, present, and future through these details.

People I Saw

Each dot is someone I engaged with. Dots that sit close together showed up in the same entries at least 3 times that year. Bigger dots appeared more often. The lines indicate the other people I also spent time with when I saw that person.

Counts are the number of entries a person appeared in each year.
